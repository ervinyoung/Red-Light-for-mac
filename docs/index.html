Warmth

Zero blue at 60%

Blue is scaled to nothing at the display's own transfer table, so text stays crisp. Past 60% it goes deeper with a small luminance-preserving tint, never a red blob.

Keyboard

0.1%, then off

A tenth of the least the system slider allows, and dark after five seconds of stillness. Sunset fades everything in over thirty minutes.

Learning

Three nights, then it knows

Adjust anything by hand and it lets you. Do it the same way on three nights and that becomes the default. On-device, one notification, one command to undo.
